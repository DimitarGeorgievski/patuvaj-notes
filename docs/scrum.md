## Three Things Scrum Guide Does Not Prescribe

1. **Daily Scrum questions:** Scrum Guide does not require Developers
   to answer three specific questions during the Daily Scrum.
   Developers decide how to structure the meeting.

2. **Estimation techniques:** Scrum Guide does not prescribe
   Story Points, Planning Poker, or any specific estimation method.
   The team can choose the technique that works best for them.

3. **Development tools:** Scrum Guide does not require specific
   project management or development tools such as Jira, Trello,
   or GitHub Projects. The Scrum Team chooses its own tools.
